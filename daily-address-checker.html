Daily Address Checker

Roz ki Shopify order files upload karein — har order ka address live postcodes.io se check hoga, ghalti khud fix hogi ya status milega, aur aakhir mein asli Excel (.xlsx) file download hogi jis mein har cheez apne alag column mein hogi. Koi CSV export nahi, koi column mix nahi. Ye tool har computer aur browser mein chalta hai — koi Muse account, koi install nahi chahiye, sirf link kholein aur kaam karein.

Status ke rules — saaf aur fixed

OK
Bhejne ke liye ready. Postcode live verify, house number, street aur town sab mojood aur pehle se theek.
FIX
Khud theek kar diya. Postcode ki space / baray haroof, ALL CAPS naam ya town, extra spaces — Fix Applied (L) mein likha hai kya badla. Ready hai.
CONFIRM
Customer se poochhein. House number missing hai, ya town us postcode ke area se match nahi karta. Bhejne se pehle confirm karein.
HOLD
Na bhejein. Postcode missing, ghalat, ya postcodes.io par mila hi nahi — ya street hi missing hai. Pehle address theek karwayein.
ROYAL MAIL PENDING
Address ready hai, lekin aaj ki queue full hai. Royal Mail queue roz sirf 50 orders leti hai aur £30+ wale orders pehle jate hain — baaki aglay din.
Shopify order files yahan drop karein, ya click karke choose karein
Ek saath kai files bhi chalengi — .csv .xlsx .xls · Shopify export ke columns (Name, Shipping Address1, Shipping City, Shipping Zip, Total waghera) khud pehchan lega.

Sample mein 55 orders hain — kuch £30 se kam, kuch zyada, 3 mein jaan boojh kar masla (postcode missing, ghalat postcode, town mismatch) — taake 50 wali queue aur saare status foran nazar aa jayen.

Export sirf .xlsx hogi. Doosri sheet “Summary” mein roz ke counts aur queue ka total hoga.

Royal Mail queue ka rule: jo orders bhejne ke liye ready hain (OK aur FIX), un mein se pehle £30 ya zyada wale (baray order pehle), phir baaki — pehle 50 aaj jayenge (column M mein “Today #number”), us se zyada walon ka status ROYAL MAIL PENDING ho jayega aur woh aglay din ki queue mein rahenge. CONFIRM aur HOLD queue mein shamil hi nahi hote.
